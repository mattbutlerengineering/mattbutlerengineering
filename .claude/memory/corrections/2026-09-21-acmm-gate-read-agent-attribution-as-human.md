## Summary

A metric whose input is generated by the thing it measures cannot grade that thing. The attribution trailer is _evidence the work was agent-authored_; reading it as evidence of human intervention inverted the gate.

The broader pattern worth carrying forward: when a gate exists to certify autonomy, maturity, or any property of the automation, audit what actually produces its input signal before believing either a pass or a fail. A gate that can only be satisfied by removing correct behaviour (here, honest commit attribution) is misdesigned, and the tell is that the improving direction requires making the repo worse.

Fixed in PR #5638. The gate now passes honestly rather than being permanently capped by its own attribution convention.
