# Launch: a tour of Availability, where you set when guests can book

## What it is

Availability is the page where a restaurant manager sets when guests can book a table. It lists every rule you have set up on one page, in the order they win.

## What it does

The page lists your rules in five rows, from regular hours to booking rules. Type any date to see the hours guests can book and which rule decided them. Regular hours are your normal week, and they apply whenever nothing else does. Special dates and holidays change single days, like shorter hours on Christmas Eve. Closures and pauses stop bookings for a day, or pause online booking for tonight.

## How it helps

You can answer what any date looks like in one glance. You change one rule without touching the rest.
