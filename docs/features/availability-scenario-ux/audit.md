# Availability configuration: scenario-led UX audit, round 2

Audited on branch `feat/availability-setup-hierarchy` @ `033586217` (PR #8511), 2026-11-08, by an independent subagent. Today pinned to Sun 8 Nov 2026, desktop 1280 wide, phone 375x812. Screenshots were taken to `/tmp/ux-audit/r2-*.png` during the audit and are not committed; each finding below also cites a file:line.

Method: real UI via Playwright on fixture venues (F-one = `mockApi` Lunch+Dinner every day; "derive" = `mockEmptyVenue` stateful venue over the F-one config, so saves round-trip and the calendar shows real service names; F-close = `mockClosures`). Interactions are counted with `countInteractions` semantics (Locator actions; a time pick = 2); each count starts at `/availability` first paint and includes the final Save/Close unless noted.

Caveats (what I did NOT see):

- The Timeline "Tonight" panel did not render in the harness (page stuck on skeletons), so L108-L111 are judged from code and strings, not a screenshot.
- Plan-all-holidays step 2 and its Save could not be exercised (the harness has no mock for the plan sheet's impact check, the Next button just spun). D27's count is Special dates, Plan all, Next, Save with defaults, a lower bound; the sheet itself was measured.
- Special dates list rows with real closures were not rendered (mock returns none). C20's total is an estimate from the opened drawer.

## 1. Scenarios the product supports today (verified in UI unless marked "strings/code")

**A. Basic week.** A1, A2 (Weekdays shortcut), A3 (Different hours on some days; walked: 8), A4 last seating (auto 45 min before close, editable), A6 empty venue shows the setup form (no explicit "not taking bookings" line). A5 interval: only per date or special-hours (ServicesEditor "Seating", `ServicesEditor.tsx:125`), not in the week form: **partial**.
**B. Several services.** B7, B8 (per-service "Different days"), B10 (overnight "Closes 1:00 AM the next day"), B9 walk-in-only: only per date / Compare services "Booking" column (`ServicesEditor.tsx:146`), not in the week form: **partial**.
**C. Reuse.** C13 named schedule on chosen days (Add a schedule…), C14 edit once, "Applies to 209 future dates" shown, C15 Copy to other days, C16 seasonal (S8), C17 Use another day's hours… / Use schedule…, C18 reach of an edit (day editor "You're changing: This date only / Every Friday from date / Everywhere "X" is used / Every regular Friday"), C19 calendar cell shows schedule name, C20 Duplicate.
**D. Holidays.** D21, D22 (two doors: Close… "One service", or Plan… special hours then Remove Lunch), D23, D25 moving holidays computed for US/CA/MX (`holidays.ts`), D27 Plan all N holidays (**partial**: 120-day horizon, see 4), D33 warning ("Thanksgiving is Thu, Nov 26 and isn't planned yet", Calendar line and Special dates list). D24 recurring fixed-date: only weekly "Repeats", no yearly: **not supported**. D28 holiday template: only via a dated schedule + Use schedule, no prix-fixe/policies: **partial**.
**E. Closing.** E37, E38 (date range / Until I reopen), E39 (Repeats), E40, E41, E42 (One section; needs floor-plan sections), E43 (Booking only: Online / Phone / Waitlist), E44 (S10), E46 (staff note + guest message + chips), E47 (count, guests, deposits, list), E48-E52 (cancel/move/keep, refunds, rebook times, "runs into closure", strings/code), E54 (Reopen dialog, strings), E55 (History in ⋯).
**H.** H72 only venue-wide "Largest party online" (no minimum, not per shift): **partial**. H74/H75 Booking rules. H76 only "Stop booking from–to" tonight (strings: `availability.tonight.stopFrom`).
**L (Host/night-of; on Timeline, not /availability).** L107-L112 exist in `TonightControl` (pause, close early at, block tables, pacing, emergency close, expires end of service day); rendered at `TimelinePage.tsx:610`. Close… → Tonight also covers L108.
**O.** O128 live preview with stranded-bookings count (Guest preview, "N reservations affected").

Not supported (excluded): pacing config (G67-71 except tonight pacing), turn times, buffers, events (J), policies/deposits (I), areas bookable per shift (F56-63), private dining (K), holiday booking-open dates (D29), copy last year's holidays (D32), annual recurring closure (D24), Google/CSV import, gen-UI setup, etc.

## 3. Ranked opportunities (Manager pain x scenario frequency)

### 1. Make "which days does this run" a one-glance control, and make new services start sensibly

- **Serves:** A3, B8, B10, C13, C20 (and S3/S1 setups).
- **Now:** B10 = 9, C13 = 8, C20 ~ 10, A3 = 8. Evidence: r2-B10-late-fri-sat-overlap.png (red "Late overlaps Dinner on Friday", Save blocked), r2-dbg-after-monday.png ("Days: Mon Change days" after one tick), `QuickSetupForm.tsx:66` (collapse in drawer), `quick-setup-draft.ts:137` (preset ignores existing services).
- **Change:** Show each service's days as seven always-visible day chips (M T W T F S S) with shortcuts Weekdays / Weekends / Fri-Sat / Every day; never collapse the picker after the first tick. A new service defaults to the shared days but a preset opens at the previous service's close (Late after Dinner), so it cannot overlap on creation; the overlap message names the fix ("Move Late to 11:00 PM?") rather than blocking silently. In New/Duplicate schedule start collapsed (name + Starting/Ending + days chips) and show the services form only after the Manager chooses "Different hours than the regular week", or prefill but fold the services into one-line summaries.
- **After:** B10 9 -> 5, C13 8 -> 5, C20 ~10 -> 6, A3 8 -> 7.
- **Risk/size:** M (shared form used by onboarding, week panel and drawers; many tests assert the current collapse).

### 2. Phone: collapse the week form and make Save reachable

- **Serves:** every week edit on a phone: A1-A4, B7-B10, S1-S4, C13/C14.
- **Now:** week panel 2,421 px, Save at y = 3,181 px (r2-PH-02-schedules-long.png, r2-PH-save measurement), preview between form and Save, ~17 controls per service, 43 controls on the first screen set.
- **Change:** Phone shows each service as one summary row ("Lunch 11:30 AM - 3:00 PM, every day Edit"); tapping expands one service at a time with its three times. A sticky bottom bar appears when there are unsaved changes: "Save" plus the one-line effect ("Dinner closes 10 PM from today"). The guest preview becomes a "What guests see" row opening a sheet. Desktop keeps the form but gets the same sticky Save once the form is longer than the viewport.
- **After:** S1-type change on phone: 6 scroll screens -> 1-2; interaction count same, but Save no longer needs a 3,000 px scroll.
- **Risk/size:** M-L (form layout, preview placement, tests for focus and announcements).

### 3. Close dialog: stop asking for what the product knows, and say what happened

- **Serves:** D22, E37-E39, E41, E43, E46 (and S5/S7).
- **Now:** D22 9, E39 11, E41 4, E43 5, E46 8. Evidence: r2-D22-ready.png / r2-D22-after.png (toast "Thu, Dec 24 closed" for Lunch only, `CloseDialog.tsx:375`), r2-E41-starting-at.png (list starts at midnight), r2-E39-to-picker.png (To opens in Feb), r2-D22-close-open.png (stray floor-plan line, `CloseStepOne.tsx:208`; "Step 1 of 2", `CloseDialog.tsx:465`), r2-E43-after.png ("Booking paused on Sun, Nov 8").
- **Change:** (a) Date chips for the next holidays and "Tonight / Tomorrow / This weekend" above the picker, using the same holiday list as Special dates. (b) Pickers open on a sensible month (From: next month with a holiday or the typed date; To: From's month), Repeats defaults to a chosen weekday sentence ("Every Monday until ..."). (c) "Starting at" lists tonight's own service times and close, from now. (d) Show "Step 1 of 2" only when step 2 exists; move the floor-plan hint onto the disabled "One section" radio. (e) Toast restates the thing: "Lunch closed on Thu, Dec 24", "Phone booking paused tonight". (f) One name: the button, title and toast all say "Pause online booking" or "Close", not both.
- **After:** D22 9 -> 5 (Close, One service + Lunch, Christmas Eve chip, Close), E39 11 -> 8, E41 stays 4 but the list is the evening's 6-8 options not 40.
- **Risk/size:** M (CloseStepOne/Dialog logic is dense and heavily tested; the content changes are small).

### 4. Holiday decisions: shared, shorter, and not a 3,700 px form

- **Serves:** D27, D33, D22, D24 (partial), D21/D23 when entered through holidays.
- **Now:** Special dates -> Plan all 6 = 3,711 px, 83 controls; ~7 steps to mark three "open as usual" and plan three; horizon 120 days (`holidays.ts:139`); "Open as usual" lives in localStorage (`open-as-usual.ts:1-2`), so other Managers/devices re-see everything. Evidence: r2-D27-plan-sheet-top.png, r2-04-special.png, r2-PH-05-special.png.
- **Change:** One line per holiday with a three-way choice **Closed / Open as usual / Special hours** (selecting the last expands hours inline). A header line "Restaurants like yours usually close for Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day: Close for these 3". "Open as usual" is saved to the venue (a reviewed-holiday record, so the Calendar nudge also clears for everyone). Horizon: through next year, grouped by month with "Show next year". Open-as-usual and Plan become full-height rows, not caption links (phone touch target).
- **After:** D27 7 -> 4 (Special dates, Close for these 3, Open as usual for the rest, Save), and no no-op "Special hours = usual hours" rules.
- **Risk/size:** M (needs a persistence field for reviewed decisions; UI is a rewrite of the plan sheet).

### 5. Copy / Use another day: say what it changes, and don't leave "Special hours" labels that aren't

- **Serves:** C15, C17, C18, A3 (when done via the calendar), D23.
- **Now:** C15 = 6 and produces "Copied to 105 dates" (`CopyDayAction.tsx` Until defaults to No end, r2-C15-copy-after.png); calendar cells read "Special hours" on dates whose hours equal the regular week; C17 = 6 with an empty date picker.
- **Change:** After choosing days, the footer states the consequence ("Wed and Thu will use Tuesday's hours from Nov 17, every week. This changes your regular week.") with a choice "Just this week / Every week (updates the regular week)"; Every week routes to the regular-week change (one source of truth, label stays "Regular"). When the copied hours equal what the date already has, say "Already the same" and save nothing. "Use another day's hours" offers weekday chips with the date's own neighbours (Sat, Sun, Mon...) before the date picker. Calendar shows "Special hours" only when hours actually differ.
- **After:** C15 6 -> 5 (Calendar, cell, Copy, Wed + Thu preselected from a chip "Wed-Thu", Copy), C17 6 -> 5.
- **Risk/size:** M (rule model: copying as "repeating special_hours" vs editing the regular week; needs equality check in the day summary).

### 6. Put tonight's state on /availability, and use one name

- **Serves:** E40, E41, L107-L111 (Manager checking before service).
- **Now:** Tonight controls live on Timeline (`TimelinePage.tsx:610`) while /availability has Close… and Pause; nothing on /availability says what is already in force tonight (paused, closing early, tables blocked). Names split: "Starting at" (Close…) vs "Close early at" (Timeline); "Tonight's Service" in the sidebar is a briefing page with fixed Early/Dinner/Late bands (r2-L108-tonight-page.png).
- **Change:** A one-line "Tonight" status row under the header (e.g. "Tonight: open as usual" / "Online paused since 6:10 PM; closing early 9:30 PM") that links to the Timeline Tonight panel and to Close…. Rename Timeline's "Close early at" to match the dialog, and rename the briefing "Tonight's Service" to "Briefing" or drive its chips from the venue's services.
- **After:** discoverability 0 -> 1 glance; E41 remains 4.
- **Risk/size:** S (read-only status + copy), S-M for the sidebar rename.

### 7. Calendar: show differences and a legend, not repeated sameness

- **Serves:** C19, C16, D21-D23, E37-E39 (verifying what was saved).
- **Now:** r2-C19-calendar-with-template.png: 28 of 35 cells read "Weeknight / Lunch - Dinner" or "Regular / Lunch - Dinner"; hours only in the day drawer or Week view; with the count-only payload every cell reads "Regular - 2 services" (r2-03-calendar.png).
- **Change:** Month cells show nothing for the regular pattern and a coloured chip for anything else (Weeknight, Closed, Special hours, Paused), with a legend line above the grid built from the schedules in use ("Weeknight Mon-Thu, Summer Jun 1 - Sep 15, 2 closures"). Hours appear in a hover/long-press card.
- **After:** "which days differ" 1 click + scan -> 0 clicks.
- **Risk/size:** S-M (cell presentation only; day-summary tests).

### 8. Surface Booking rules where the Manager looks

- **Serves:** H72, H74, H75.
- **Now:** 4 steps via ⋯; the same facts are a non-interactive line at the bottom of Schedules (`SchedulesTab.tsx:263`), below Other schedules (r2-01-schedules.png).
- **Change:** Make that line a button directly under the week form ("Guests can book up to 30 days ahead, 1 hour notice, parties up to 12 online. Change"). Leaves room for per-service party size later.
- **After:** 4 -> 3.
- **Risk/size:** S.
