Add three longer duration tokens for expressive motion: `--rialto-duration-scene` (0.6s), `--rialto-duration-cinematic` (1.2s) and `--rialto-duration-ambient` (8s). The reduced-motion adapter collapses all three to `0s` along with the existing duration tokens.
